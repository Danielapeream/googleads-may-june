01 · Quick context

Where this plan comes from and what it solves.

The prior campaign analysis identified three concurrent problems: 15% of spend is evaporating across 8 ad groups with zero conversions, the current copy is poorly written and poorly positioned (keyword stuffing, typos like "Ltl", vague value proposition), and the landing pages are the root cause hurting both Quality Score and organic SEO at the same time.

This document resolves the second problem (copy) and sequences the resolution of the third (landing pages). The ad group pauses and relaunches in this plan are aligned with that sequence.

05 · Deployment plan

Three weeks, three execution blocks.

The plan is sequenced by dependency: anything that can be launched without touching the website goes first; anything that needs a new LP follows. If everything goes to plan, by Week 4 all 10 ad groups are running with new copy and relevant landing pages.

Week 1 · This week
Quick wins, no product changes
  • Pause the 8 ad groups with zero conversions (TForce, ABF, SAIA, XPO, Vitran, Estes, Roadrunner, FedEx)
  • Launch new copy in Generic LTL — RSA A and B in parallel
  • Launch new copy in Day & Ross and Manitoulin — replaces current copy
  • Revert bid strategy to "Maximize Conversions" until we hit 40+ conv/month
  • Activate extensions: sitelinks, callouts, structured snippets
Week 2
Build the landing base
  • Build /ltl-carriers — interim comparison page
  • Build /content/tforce and /content/abf — priority based on pre-pause volume
  • Audit remaining /content/* pages that are indexed but empty
  • First performance check-in on Week 1 ads
Weeks 3 & 4
Relaunch carriers, scale winners
  • Relaunch TForce and ABF with new copy and LPs (Week 3)
  • Build remaining LPs: SAIA, XPO, Estes, FedEx Freight, Roadrunner, Vitran
  • Relaunch the remaining 6 ad groups as each LP is ready (Week 4)
  • Scale budget on the "Comparison" ad group (66× ROAS, underfunded)
  • Decide whether Roadrunner relaunches or consolidates into Comparison

06 · Decisions required

Four approvals to start executing.

What I need from Chris to kick off

  1. Approve the 3-week deployment plan as defined. If there are specific objections to any phase, let's align before anything goes live in Google Ads.
  2. Validate with product that the 9 listed carriers are active on the platform. If any aren't offered, they come out of the plan.
  3. Approve the Generic LTL copy for A/B testing. If Chris prefers a specific variant of the two, flag it. Default: both in parallel.
  4. Confirm ownership of Google Ads changes: copy, ad group, and bid strategy changes go through marketing first, with a weekly check-in with Chris to review performance.
Risk of not executing

Keeping the current Generic LTL copy ("Freight Billing" in the headline) continues to position FreightSimple as a billing tool, not as an LTL quoting platform. Every day without change = wrong advertising signal being learned by Google's algorithm.

Once approved, Phase 1 can start this same week. The two new Generic LTL RSAs can be live in under 30 minutes from approval.
