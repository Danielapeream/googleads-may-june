02 · Rollout

Three weeks, three execution blocks.

The plan is sequenced by dependency: what can launch without touching the website goes first, what needs a new landing page follows.

NowWeek 1
Quick wins, no product changes
  • Pause 8 ad groups with zero conversions
  • Launch new copy in Generic LTL (A + B test)
  • Launch new copy in Day & Ross and Manitoulin
  • Revert bid strategy to Maximize Conversions
  • Activate sitelinks, callouts, snippets
Week 2
Build the landing base
  • Build /ltl-carriers interim comparison page
  • Build /content/tforce and /content/abf
  • Audit empty /content/* pages
  • First performance check-in of Week 1 ads
Weeks 3 & 4
Relaunch carriers, scale winners
  • Relaunch TForce, ABF, Vitran (W3)
  • Build remaining LPs: SAIA, XPO, Estes, FedEx, Roadrunner
  • Relaunch remaining 6 ad groups (W4)
  • Scale budget on "Comparison" ad group
